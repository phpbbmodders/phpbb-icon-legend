[size=150][b][u]WHAT THE ICONS MEAN[/u][/b][/size]

A few general points:
[list]
[*][b]Red[/b] icons mean unread posts; [b]blue/grey[/b] icons mean everything has been read.
[*]A [b]star[/b] on a topic icon means you have posted in that topic.
[*]A [b]hot[/b] topic is one with a lot of posts. The default is 25; administrators can change it under ACP » General » Post settings » Popular topic threshold.
[/list]

{{SMALL_ICONS}}
The small picture some topics have before their title (smiley, lightbulb, arrow and so on) is a topic icon. The poster picks it when posting, if the forum allows topic icons, and it doesn't mean anything about read or unread status.

Colours and shapes are for the default prosilver style; other styles may look different.
